01 · OverviewSpecified

Collara documentation

Collara is a private coordination workspace for used CNC equipment financing. It links an equipment passport with its evidence, verification scope, lender decision, and collateral workflow, and discloses each record only to the counterparties named on it. It is being designed as Daml workflows on Canton.

Project status: pre-build

What exists today is a product specification and three interactive UI mockups: the landing page, the lender workspace, and a governance simulation inside that workspace. The mockups project state locally in the browser. There are no deployed contracts, no ledger, no API, and no test suite. Nothing on this page describes working software.

What Collara coordinates
  • Equipment passport and document versions
  • Verification requests and scoped attestations
  • Evidence packages shared with a named lender
  • Lender assessment and collateral decision
  • Pledge activation, release request, and release decision
  • Permission-scoped case history and exports
What Collara is not
  • Not a lender; credit decisions and funding stay with the lender
  • Not a custodian and not a legal lien registry
  • An attestation is not proof of legal ownership
  • A Collara record does not verify pledges made outside Collara
  • Cash settlement and MainNet wallet payments are outside the initial scope

02 · WorkflowSpecified

From equipment evidence to authorized release

Six steps, each ending in a committed workflow state. Operational events such as uploads, drafts, and views are application actions and do not change workflow state. Failed or unknown commands never appear as successful lifecycle events.

Pledge activation prerequisites (all required)
  1. Lender-authorized collateral decision: Eligible for this case
  2. Proposal issued and accepted at the exact version
  3. Attestation valid at activation
  4. Evidence snapshot matches the attested versions
  5. Asset control available: no active lock on the canonical control
Workflow rules
  • One active lock per registered asset within Collara
  • Eligibility is recorded per lender and per case; it does not transfer
  • Only the designated lender's approver mandate can authorize release
  • A rejected release leaves the lock active; the owner may reapply
  • Release ends the Collara workflow lock only; legal lien termination is separate
  • Historical lock records are retained after release

Boundary. Credit decisions, disbursement, legal filings, and enforcement remain with the lender and its existing processes. The workflow blocks a second active lock for the same registered asset within Collara; it does not detect pledges made outside the system or guarantee that duplicate registrations of a physical asset cannot occur.

03 · Roles & permissionsSpecified

Different participants, different permissions

Participation does not grant access to every document, every loan term, or every case. Responsibilities are expressed as mandates in the workflow, not only as interface controls. Access is re-evaluated when a report is generated and again when it is downloaded.

Equipment owner
Borrower mandate

Registers the passport, submits evidence, requests verification, approves sharing, accepts the proposal, co-authorizes activation, and requests release.

Dealer
Contributor

Contributes equipment records to an invited case and consents when those records are shared onward.

Verifier
Registry member

Accepts an assignment, requests changes, and issues an attestation with an explicit scope. Cannot approve financing or establish legal lien priority.

Lender analyst
Lender · Analyst mandate

Works the review queue, opens shared evidence, drafts and submits the assessment, and requests exports. Cannot record decisions.

Lender approver
Lender · Approver mandate

Records the collateral decision, issues or withdraws proposals, co-authorizes activation, and authorizes or rejects release. Holds the organization's governance seat (planned).

Auditor
Scoped grant

Inspects and exports the records covered by a grant that names the case, the permission, the expiry, and the consenting parties.

05 · Planned BitSafe governancePlannedUI simulation

Verifier-registry administration by a governance set

The BitSafe governance module is intended to decide which verifiers can be assigned to verification requests, so that no single operator adds or suspends a verifier. It is a planned capability. The workspace contains a UI simulation of it until the Decentralization Manager integration is implemented.

Governance controls verifier-registry administration only. It never authorizes collateral release. Collateral decisions, financing proposals, pledge activation, and release remain under lender mandates and are not subject to governance votes.
