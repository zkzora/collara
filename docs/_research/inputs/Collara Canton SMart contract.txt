
Collara Canton/Daml Contract Structure
Overview
Dokumen ini menjelaskan struktur contract pada Collara menggunakan Canton Network dan Daml.Collara menggunakan model distributed ledger berbasis contract untuk mengelola identitas aset, verifikasi, collateral assessment, pembiayaan, dan audit.
AssetPassport Contract
Tujuan:Merepresentasikan identitas digital aset fisik.Data:- Asset ID- Asset category- Manufacturer- Serial number- Owner- Purchase value- Current valuation- Location- Asset status- Creation dateSignatory:Asset OwnerObservers:Verifier, Bank, AuditorLifecycle:Created → Verified → Active → Transferred → Archived
Verification Contract
Tujuan:Menyimpan proses pemeriksaan dan validasi aset.Data:- Asset reference- Verification provider- Inspection result- Condition score- Supporting documents- Approval statusSignatory:VerifierObservers:Asset Owner, Financial Institution, AuditorLifecycle:Requested → Review → Approved/Rejected
CollateralAssessment Contract
Tujuan:Menentukan apakah aset memenuhi syarat sebagai collateral.Data:- Asset reference- Lender- Valuation- Risk score- Eligibility statusSignatory:Financial InstitutionLifecycle:Submitted → Evaluated → Eligible/Rejected
Financing Contract
Tujuan:Mencatat hubungan pembiayaan antara lender dan pemilik aset.Data:- Loan ID- Borrower- Lender- Asset collateral- Amount- Duration- StatusLifecycle:Requested → Approved → Active → Completed
AuditRecord Contract
Tujuan:Mencatat seluruh aktivitas sistem.Data:- Event- Actor- Timestamp- Previous state- New stateAudit record dibuat setiap terjadi perubahan contract.
